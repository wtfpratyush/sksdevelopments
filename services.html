Questions

Services FAQ

Do you handle design as well as building?

Yes. We can produce drawings in-house or work with your architect or designer — either way, one team takes responsibility for the build.

Can you combine several services in one project?

Most of our projects do — an extension with a new kitchen, or a renovation with bespoke carpentry. One quote, one schedule, one team.

Do you do small jobs?

We take on everything from a single structural wall to a full new home. If it’s too small for us, we’ll tell you honestly and point you to someone good.

Are your quotes fixed?

Your written quote sets out exactly what’s included. If you ask for changes, they’re priced and agreed in writing before work starts.
