Licences & Warranty

Licensing, warranty and the partners we work with.

Licences, warranty and affiliations

We build to the standards British Columbia expects of residential builders, and we’re happy to show you the paperwork before you commit.

Residential builder licensing

New homes in British Columbia must be built by a builder licensed through BC Housing’s Licensing & Consumer Services. Our licence details are available on request.

Home warranty

New homes are covered by third-party home warranty insurance on the provincial 2-5-10 model: two years on materials and labour, five years on the building envelope and ten years on structure.

Energy Step Code

We design and build to the BC Energy Step Code level your municipality requires, so new homes are efficient, comfortable and ready for inspection.

Insurance

We carry liability insurance and WorkSafeBC coverage. Certificates are available on request.
