Questions

Before You Get in Touch

Is the site visit really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the property and talk through options with no charge and no obligation.

What should I send you?

Your address, a few photos and a rough idea of what you want. Drawings help if you have them, but they’re not needed.

How soon can you start?

It depends on design and permit time for your project. We’ll give you realistic dates in your quote.

Which areas do you cover?

Surrey and across the Lower Mainland. If you’re a little further out, call us — we often travel for the right project.
