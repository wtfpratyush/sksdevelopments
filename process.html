Typical Timelines

How Long Things
Usually Take

Every home is different — your quote includes a programme for your project. Permit times vary by municipality.

Kitchen fitting

Usually 3–6 weeks on site once materials are ready.

Loft or garage conversion

Usually 6–10 weeks, depending on structure and permits.

Home extension

Usually 3–5 months on site, plus design and permit time.

New custom home

Usually 10–16 months from permit to keys.

Questions

Process FAQ

Do I need a permit for my project?

Most extensions, structural changes, conversions and new builds need a building permit. We check what your municipality requires during the site visit and handle the application for you.

Can we stay at home while you work?

Often yes, especially for extensions and kitchen fits. We plan the work to keep your home usable and tell you in advance about any days with no water or power.

How are payments scheduled?

Payments are tied to clear milestones set out in your contract, so you only pay as work is completed.

Who do I talk to during the build?

You get one site lead for day-to-day questions, plus direct access to us by phone or email.

What happens if something changes?

Changes are priced and agreed in writing before the work is done, so the budget never drifts quietly.
